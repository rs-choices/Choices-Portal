# Choices

Choices helps people decide what to do with their free time. It brings
restaurants, cafés, activities, events, leisure spots and live offers into one
app, learns what each person likes, and suggests places that fit their taste,
budget, mood and location. When someone cannot make up their mind, Choices
narrows the options down for them, down to a single swipe or a planned day out.

## The problem

Deciding where to go is harder than it should be. The information is spread
across Instagram, Google Maps, event listings, WhatsApp groups and venue
websites, and none of it knows what you personally enjoy. People scroll, compare
and often end up going nowhere, or back to the same place as always.

Venues have the opposite problem: they have offers, events and quiet hours to
fill, but no simple way to reach the nearby people who would actually like them.

## The solution

Choices sits between the two.

- **For people going out (clients)**, a free mobile app. They tell Choices what
  they like once, then get matched suggestions, a swipe deck of today's picks,
  and a planner that builds a whole day out as a route on a map.
- **For venues (business owners)**, a web portal. They keep their listing up to
  date, post offers and events, reply to reviews and see how people discover
  them.

## Who uses it

| User | Product | What they want |
|---|---|---|
| Client | Choices mobile app | Something good to do nearby, without the effort of deciding |
| Business owner (and their team) | Choices Business Portal | More of the right customers, and proof that Choices brings them |
| Choices team | Internal tools (not designed yet) | Manage public spots, support users and venues |

## Market

Choices launches in the **United Arab Emirates**. Prices are in AED and the
portal offers English and Arabic. The client app designs use Lebanese sample
content (Beirut, Batroun, +961 numbers, US$ prices); that content is
placeholder and will be replaced with UAE places.

## How it makes money

Not decided yet. The working assumption is a **monthly subscription paid by
venues**, while the client app stays free. The portal design uses three plans:

| Plan | Price | Main benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | AED 299 / month | Listing, up to 10 active offers, dashboard |
| Growth | AED 799 / month | Unlimited offers, analytics, priority placement |
| Enterprise | Custom | Multiple locations, account manager, API access |

These prices come from the portal design and are provisional.
