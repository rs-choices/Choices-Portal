# Suggestions

Ideas that are **not** in the current designs. Nothing here is planned or
approved; the feature documents do not depend on any of it. Each idea is kept
here so it can be discussed and either promoted into a design or dropped.

The three with the most impact are **7**, **2** and **4**.

## Details

### 1. "Just pick for me"

One button that returns a single answer: the best-matching place that is open
right now, near the client, within their spending range. The swipe deck still
asks the client to choose; this chooses for them, which is the core promise of
the app.

### 2. Group decision

Friends join the same swipe deck from their own phones, and the app shows the
places everyone swiped right on. The trip planner already asks "With who?" and
can invite friends, so this extends an existing idea.

### 3. Mood-first entry

Quick chips on Home, such as "Chill", "Date night", "With kids" or
"Adrenaline", that fill in the search and filters in one tap, instead of the
open "What are you in the mood for?" field.

### 4. Arabic and right-to-left layout

An Arabic version of the client app with a mirrored layout. The portal already
offers English and العربية; the app designs are English only.

### 5. Weather- and season-aware picks

Use the weather and time of year to favour indoor places in the summer and
outdoor ones in the cooler months. Places are already tagged indoor and
outdoor.

### 6. Ramadan mode

During Ramadan, show iftar and suhoor offers and each venue's changed opening
hours. In the portal, let owners set Ramadan hours and tag offers for iftar or
suhoor.

### 7. In-app booking

Let clients pick a date, time and party size in the app. The booking arrives in
the portal's Bookings section, where the owner confirms it and checks the
client in. It would give venues proof of real visits, which is the best
argument for paying a subscription. This is also open question 1 in
[shared-model.md](shared-model.md).

### 8. Offer check-in or redemption code

When a client uses an offer, the app shows a code that staff enter or scan in
the portal. It gives the portal's "claims" a real meaning and tells the venue
the offer brought someone through the door.

### 9. "Why you're seeing this"

A short line on each suggestion, such as "Matches: sushi, outdoor, mid-range".
Clients trust the match score more, and owners learn which tags bring them
visitors.

### 10. Post-visit prompt

After a client likely visited a place (a trip stop, a WhatsApp chat, a
reminder), ask "Did you go to Roadsters?" and offer a quick rating. It gives
the Energy map, reviews and portal analytics real visit data instead of only
swipes.
